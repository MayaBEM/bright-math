Day Challenge

5 questions a day, SEAMO-style, Grades 3 and 4. Do all 5 questions, then ask a grown-up to check.

For parents and teachersYour child does one day (5 questions) at a time. Each question has a scratch pad for working, or use paper. When all 5 answers are typed, your child presses “I'm done”. Later, press “Parent check” and enter the code. The app marks each answer, shows the time spent on each question (your child never sees a timer) and opens a step-by-step solution.
