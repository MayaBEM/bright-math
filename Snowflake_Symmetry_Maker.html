What is a line of symmetry?

A line of symmetry cuts a shape into two halves that match exactly. If you fold the shape on the line, one half lands right on top of the other.

Each half is a reflection (a mirror image) of the other half.

Fold the heart: the halves match
Fold the mitten: the halves do not match
