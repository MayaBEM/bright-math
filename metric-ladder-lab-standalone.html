Why it works

Powers of ten

Every extra step on the ladder just adds another set of three zeros — that's all a "power of ten" is.

  • Smaller unit = multiply (number gets bigger). Bigger unit = divide (number gets smaller).
  • Every step is exactly ×1000 or ÷1000 — never ×100 or ×10.
  • ×1000 or ÷1000 always shifts the decimal point 3 places, once per step.
