💡 Memory Tricks

  • Acute = A cute little angle → the small one, less than 90°
  • Right angle = a square 90° corner (think of a book or a wall corner)
  • Obtuse = the “wide” one, more than 90° but not yet a straight 180°
  • Reflex = the “bent-back” one, more than 180° (past a half-turn)
  • Complementary → C = Corner (90°) · Supplementary → S = Straight (180°)
  • Vertical angles sit opposite each other in an “X” and are always equal
